## Limitations

<!-- Lead notes for H1; final wording and numbers come from artifacts/. -->
- Cover in practice requires a registered witness device. Index-only payment (R2) needs at least `theta_hi` of a county's customers out; `theta_hi` sits at the edge of the search grid with no interior optimum, so R2 almost never pays.
- Outages affecting less than `theta_lo` of a county are not covered, even if the member's premises is dark. This is a limit of county-level data.
- Self-reported witness entries (from a browser without the Battery Status API) are weaker evidence than sensed charger events, though the rules treat them the same.
- The 0.10 rate at which a witness wrongly confirms a payment is an assumption set by the team, not a measured value. The trigger artifact reports how the chosen thresholds change across 0.05, 0.10 and 0.20.
